# Third-party names and artwork

## Service names

Token Grill uses the names of the services that it monitors: Codex, Claude,
Kiro, and Antigravity. It uses these names only to show the account of each
value. These names are trademarks of their owners. Token Grill is an
independent project. The owners of these services do not endorse or sponsor
Token Grill.

## Service icons

The service icons in `resources/icons/providers/` are original symbolic
drawings for Token Grill. They are not copies or tracings of the logos of the
services. The project license applies to these icons.

## Screenshots

The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` show the desktop background
`wallhaven-mlykjm` from wallhaven.cc. The author of that image keeps its
copyright. The screenshots also show the Dash dock of the Dhruva extension
and the blur of the Blur My Shell extension. These extensions are not part of
Token Grill.
